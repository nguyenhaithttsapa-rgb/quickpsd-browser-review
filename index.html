2. Adobe Creative Cloud Web Viewer

Rating: 8.2 / 10

Adobe offers an official web viewer for files synced to Creative Cloud. While accurate for colors, it requires both parties to maintain Adobe accounts and has limited layer manipulation compared to standalone suites.

3. WebAssembly GIMP Port

Rating: 7.5 / 10

Open-source ports of GIMP compile into Wasm for the web. While functional, they feature an outdated UI, steep learning curve, and lack native Smart Object compatibility.
